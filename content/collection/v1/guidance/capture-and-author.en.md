# How to write and photograph your pages

Status: draft pending owner review. Participant-facing guidance for collection protocol v1.

## Before you start

- Only take part with **your own handwriting**. Do not photograph someone else's writing, letters, forms or signatures.
- You must be an adult to take part. We ask you to confirm this; we never guess anyone's age from their handwriting.
- Taking part in each use is a separate choice. Saying no to one does not affect the others, and any compensation does not depend on your choices or on how your handwriting looks.

## Writing

- Use one sheet of paper per task. Plain white paper is best; lined or squared paper is fine if you tell us which you used.
- Use the pen or pencil you normally write with, and tell us what kind it is.
- Write on a firm surface in your usual position and at your usual speed. There is no "good" or "bad" handwriting here.
- Keep copied text and free writing on **separate pages**. Do not add your name, a signature or any personal details.

## Photographing

- Place the page on a plain, darker surface in even light. Avoid shadows from your hand or phone, and do not use the flash.
- Hold the phone roughly straight above the page so the page does not look like a trapezoid.
- Make sure **all four corners** of the page are visible. Do not crop the edges.
- Do not use filters, "scan" enhancement, beauty modes or document-cleanup apps. Send the photo as it was taken.

## Repeat photos and second sessions

- We may ask you to photograph the **same page again** after moving the phone. That tells us how much the photo itself changes the measurements. It is still the same page by the same person, not a new sample.
- Some participants may be invited to write the tasks again on another day. That is a new page by the same person, and it is never counted as a different person.

## Changing your mind

- You can withdraw any permission at any time through the channel given in your participation agreement. Withdrawal stops future use and starts deletion from our systems as described in the agreement. It cannot recall copies that already left our systems in a way the agreement allowed.
