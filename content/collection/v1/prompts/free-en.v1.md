# Free-writing task — English (`free_en`, version 1)

Status: draft pending owner review. Original text written for this project.

**Instructions for the writer**

Describe an everyday object you can see from where you are sitting, for example a cup, a chair or a lamp. What does it look like, what is it used for, and how did it end up where it is?

Write at least five lines on one page in your normal handwriting. There are no right or wrong answers, and spelling does not matter.

Please do **not** write names, addresses, phone numbers, health information, private messages or anything else you would not want someone else to read. Do not add a signature.
